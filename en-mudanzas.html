Moving FAQs

Do you charge by the hour or by the job?

We prefer to give you a flat price for the complete job, once we know what has to be moved and the distances involved.

Can you move appliances and large furniture?

Yes: refrigerators, washers, sofas, beds and assembled furniture. If anything has to be taken apart, we tell you beforehand.

Do you travel outside Jacksonville?

Yes, we cover up to 100 miles from Jacksonville: Orange Park, Saint Augustine, Fernandina Beach and more.
