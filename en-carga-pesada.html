High-volume load FAQs

What kind of load can you NOT carry?

Loads that go beyond legal weight limits or that need a special permit we cannot process in time. If something cannot be done, we tell you straight before charging you.

Do you handle loads that need a crane or forklift to unload?

We coordinate with the crane company or the customer's forklift at the delivery point.

How much does a heavy load cost?

It depends on the weight, the distance and whether waiting to unload is needed. With that information we give you the price in writing.
