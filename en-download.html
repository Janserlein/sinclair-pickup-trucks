1. Choose your phone

DOWNLOAD FOR ANDROID

APK file (about 80 MB). This is the full app.

On iPhone you use it in the browser and can add it to your home screen.

2. Install on Android (important step)

Android may warn that the app is "not from Play Store". That is normal: it is installed directly from our page. Do this once:
  1. Open the downloaded file (bottom notification or Downloads).
  2. If asked, tap Settings and allow "Install from this source".
  3. If Google Play Protect blocks it:
    • Open the Play Store app.
    • Tap your photo at the top right.
    • Open Play Protect → ⚙ (top right) → turn off "Scan apps…".
    • Go back and install the app.
    • When done, you may turn it back on.
  4. Tap Install and open it.
  5. Sign in with your phone and the 6-digit code sent to your email.

3. Install on iPhone

  1. Tap OPEN ON iPHONE above (it opens in Safari).
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen" and name it SP TRUCKS.
  4. You now have the icon on your phone, like an app.
